Nippon Fit for Parents

Everything about your child at the dojo, in one place on your phone.

How to get started

Five steps, about two minutes. There is nothing to download from an app store.

You will be able to see your child's belt, their attendance month by month, what fees are due, when they are put forward for grading, and every medal they have won.

STEP 1

Open it

On your phone, open your browser and go to:

app.nipponfit.com

Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone.

STEP 2

Sign in

  • Mobile number — the one you gave the dojo. Ten digits only, no +91 and no spaces.
  • Password — nkc2026 in small letters.

Both parents can have their own login if you have given us both numbers. You will both see the same child.

STEP 3

Change your password

Everyone starts on the same password, so please change it straight away.

Tap Account → Change your password → type a new one twice → Change my password.

If you forget it, call the dojo and we will reset it. We cannot see your password — we can only replace it.

STEP 4

Put it on your home screen

This makes it open like a normal app, and on an iPhone it is required before reminders will work.

Android · Chrome

Tap the ⋮ menu, top right → Add to Home screen → Add

iPhone · Safari

Tap Share at the bottom (a square with an arrow) → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add

From then on, open it from the new icon rather than the browser.

STEP 5

Turn on reminders

So your phone tells you when a fee is due, instead of you having to remember.

Tap Account → Reminders → Send reminders to this phone → Allow.

On an iPhone this only appears once you have finished step 4 and opened the app from the home screen icon. That is an Apple rule, not ours.

What you will see

The My child screen

Belt

The grade your child holds now, and the next one up.

Attendance

This month's figure. Tap it to open every month since your child joined — how many classes they came to, how many their fee covers, and the percentage. Grading opens at 75%.

Fees

What is due, when, and what it is made up of. A Pay by UPI button opens GPay or PhonePe with the amount and your child's ID card number already filled in — or pay at the dojo as usual. Once we mark it received, the screen says so.

Grading

When the instructor puts your child forward, the form appears here with their details already filled in — name, date of birth, ID card number, current belt. You check it and submit.

Belt history and medals

Every grading and every tournament result, kept for as long as they train with us.

More than one child with us? Buttons at the top switch between them.

Worth knowing

Two children, ₹1,000 off each

If two or more of your children train with us, ₹1,000 comes off every one of their fees automatically. You will see it listed on the fee breakdown.

Taking a break?

Tell us and we will mark it. You will not be billed or chased while your child is away, those months are not counted against their attendance, and their place and their whole history are kept.

Some months show more classes than your plan

Extra training is welcome, and simply counts as full attendance. The current month counts only up to today, so it looks smaller than a finished month.

If something does not work

“That mobile number and password do not match”

Check the ten digits have no spaces, and that the password is nkc2026 in small letters. If it still refuses, you may be listed under a different number — call us.

The screen looks stuck or out of date

Go to app.nipponfit.com/?reset — that clears it and starts fresh. Nothing is lost.

Anything else at all

Call the dojo. We would much rather you rang than gave up on it.

Call the dojo · 9945616005 Monday to Sunday, during class hours

Your privacy

You can only ever see your own child — not other children, not other parents' details. That is enforced by the system itself, not by hiding buttons.

Instructors see names and belts so they can mark attendance. They cannot see your phone number, your email, or anything about fees.
